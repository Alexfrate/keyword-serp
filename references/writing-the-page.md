# Writing the page (do not wait to be reminded)

Do all of this in one pass, before you say the page is live. The query is already chosen.

## Shape

One question, one URL. Two different searches do not share a page (the cost of making a video is not the cost of airing it or paying for clicks).

Read organic page 1 for that exact phrase. Copy the shape of the answer, not their words, and not their prices as if you measured them. No competitor names. No links out to them.

If the question is a price: the numbers come first, in a market table from cheapest to dearest. The business is not a row. Its real price, if one is published, sits under the table in the same bordered card the site already uses. If there is no list price, say the total is agreed before starting. Do not invent a fee. The line above the table says these figures are found around, not ours. The first screen has no prices.

Then explain. Each block says something new. Three sections that repeat the same three warnings get cut. If the real work is a specific method (for example the clip is made with AI from the customer's own photo), the page says that in the body. The title stays the question people search. Do not describe a different trade just because page 1 talks about crews and sets. Do not name the software.

## Weight

Before publishing, count the body words and compare them with whoever on page 1 answers the same question. Stay spoken and professional. Do not clone a multi-thousand-word guide and do not stay a flyer. If you are under the short end, add what they explain and you do not. If the extra words are a repeat, cut them.

## Cards

Explanations are cards, not a wall of paragraphs. Same card style as the page: white, border, wide corners.

- Two or four cards: two per row.
- Three cards: three equal columns. Never a half row with an empty gap on the right.
- Five: the last card spans the row, or you merge. Do not leave a hole.
- Phone: one under the other.

The rule that stacks the grids goes at the end of the CSS. If the desktop grid is written later, it wins on a phone too. A name that must stay whole is kept with a forced line break and a non-breaking space. Check around 390 pixels: no sideways scroll.

The contact pair is the one the site already uses. On Lab: blue WhatsApp and a white "write to us" button to the contact page. Do not invent another label.

## After save

The grey Google line is the same sentence in the meta description, Open Graph, and Twitter. The title is the question plus the site name.

On Elementor, updating the HTML wipes Yoast. Put it back after, clear the Elementor cache, and purge that URL. Do not say it is live until the public HTML shows the new sentence.

FAQ schema only if those questions are visible on the page.
